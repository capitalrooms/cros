## 1. TENANTS

**Subject:** Your Capital Rooms app is coming. Here's how to get ready
**Preheader:** Everything for your home in one app: repairs, rent, housemates and more.

Hello {{first_name}},

We're moving everything for your home into one app. It's quicker than email, and you can see what's happening at any time.

**Before you start, have ready:**
- Your sign-in email address (the one we invited you with)
- A password you'll remember

**Getting started (about 5 minutes):**
1. Install the app: follow the steps above for your phone.
2. Open it and sign in.
3. Go through the four welcome screens. You can skip them and come back later.

**Everyday things**
- **Home.** Your room, your rent and its due date, your next visits and cleans, and the notice board at a glance.
- **Repairs.** Report a repair with photos, see its status, and open your past requests under My requests.
- **Book a visit.** Ask for a visit from the office or a contractor when you need one, and see it on your calendar.
- **Emergency.** A quick route for urgent problems. For fire, gas or flooding, call 999 first.
- **Ask.** Ask anything about the house: bin days, the WiFi, rent, or who bought what for the house.

**The house**
- **Notice board.** Updates and house tasks, posted by the office or housemates.
- **House jobs.** Tick off jobs such as the bins, so housemates know they're done. The list shows who did each job last.
- **Who's who.** See who lives here, and add your own short profile.
- **My profile.** Add a photo and a line about yourself, so housemates know who you are.
- **Contributions.** Log things you've done for the house, such as a clean or a shopping run. It's shared with housemates, but totals aren't ranked.
- **My record.** Keep a private note of what you've bought for the house. Only you can see it. We don't pay anyone back.
- **Cleaner extras.** Ask the cleaner for an extra job, such as the oven, after a clean.
- **Raise a concern.** Send a private message to the office about your housemates. It's handled confidentially.

**Your home and your paperwork**
- **House information.** The WiFi, the key safe and other practical details, in one place.
- **Documents.** Your agreement and other documents, ready to download.
- **Inbox.** Messages from the office, in one list.
- **Important notes.** Things you need to read and acknowledge.
- **Safety checks.** The monthly checks for the house, and what to do if something needs fixing.
- **House guides.** Short guides on fire safety, mould and condensation, bins, being a good housemate, and more.

**Your tenancy**
- **Give notice.** Tell us you're leaving. It's a rolling tenancy, so you give two months' notice.
- **Early move-out.** Ask to leave early, and we'll talk through the options with you.
- **Rescind notice.** Change your mind about leaving, as long as it's still possible.

**Give us feedback.** Tell us what's working and what isn't, so we can improve the app and the house.

If you have any trouble, reply to this email or call 0207 112 9163.

Thank you,
The Capital Rooms team

## 3. CONTRACTORS

**Subject:** Your jobs, quotes and invoices: the Capital Rooms app
**Preheader:** See your jobs, send quotes, book visits and invoice in a few taps.

Hello {{first_name}},

Here's how to use the Capital Rooms app for your work with us.

**Before you start, have ready:**
- Your sign-in email address (the one we invited you with)
- Your password, or use "Forgot password" to set one

**Getting started:**
1. Install the app: follow the steps above for your phone.
2. Sign in. Your jobs appear on the home screen.

**What you can do:**
- **See your jobs.** Today, booked and waiting jobs are listed with the address and what's needed.
- **Go to a job.** Tap "On my way" when you leave, "I'm here" when you arrive, and record what you did.
- **Come back to a job.** If you have to leave, tap "Need to come back" and book the return visit. Jobs you've left stay under Ongoing, so you can pick them up.
- **Raise a quote.** Tap Quotes, then Raise a quote, for work you've discussed or spotted on site. The office will approve it before it goes any further.
- **Book a visit.** If you need to check something first, such as the size of a part, tap Book a visit. The office will book it and tell the tenants.
- **Invoice.** Tap Invoices to make an invoice. You can add your own reference, a note, the products you bought and receipt photos. Tap "Tidy my wording" if you'd like your note written more clearly.

**Please remember:** for fire, gas or flooding, call 999 first.

If you have any trouble, reply to this email or call 0207 112 9163.

Thank you,
The Capital Rooms team
