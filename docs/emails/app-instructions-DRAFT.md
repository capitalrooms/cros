## 1. TENANTS

**Subject:** Your Capital Rooms app is coming. Here's how to get ready
**Preheader:** Repairs, rent, your notice board and your housemates, all in one place.

Hello {{first_name}},

We're moving everything for your home into one app. It's quicker than email, and you can see what's happening at any time.

**Before you start, have ready:**
- Your sign-in email address (the one we invited you with)
- A password you'll remember

**Getting started (about 5 minutes):**
1. Install the app: follow the steps above for your phone.
2. Open it and sign in.
3. Go through the four welcome screens. You can skip them and come back to them later.

**What you can do:**
- **Report a repair.** Tap Repairs, then Report a repair. Describe the problem and add photos. We'll arrange a contractor and tell you when they're coming.
- **See your rent and dates.** The home screen shows your next rent date and any visits booked.
- **Read the notice board.** Tap House, then Notice board, for updates and house tasks.
- **Meet your housemates.** Tap House to see who lives here, and add a short profile if you want to.
- **Tick off house jobs.** Under House, tap The house. Mark the jobs you've done, such as the bins, so housemates know they're done.
- **Keep a record of what you buy for the house.** This is private, and only you can see it. We don't pay anyone back.
- **Ask anything.** Tap Ask and ask about bin days, the WiFi or rent. You'll get an answer straight away.

**Please remember:** for fire, gas or flooding, call 999 first.

If you have any trouble, reply to this email or call 0207 112 9163.

Thank you,
The Capital Rooms team

## 3. CONTRACTORS

**Subject:** Your jobs, quotes and invoices: the Capital Rooms app
**Preheader:** See your jobs, send quotes, book visits and invoice in a few taps.

Hello {{first_name}},

Here's how to use the Capital Rooms app for your work with us.

**Before you start, have ready:**
- Your sign-in email address (the one we invited you with)
- Your password, or use "Forgot password" to set one

**Getting started:**
1. Install the app: follow the steps above for your phone.
2. Sign in. Your jobs appear on the home screen.

**What you can do:**
- **See your jobs.** Today, booked and waiting jobs are listed with the address and what's needed.
- **Go to a job.** Tap "On my way" when you leave, "I'm here" when you arrive, and record what you did.
- **Come back to a job.** If you have to leave, tap "Need to come back" and book the return visit. Jobs you've left stay under Ongoing, so you can pick them up.
- **Raise a quote.** Tap Quotes, then Raise a quote, for work you've discussed or spotted on site. The office will approve it before it goes any further.
- **Book a visit.** If you need to check something first, such as the size of a part, tap Book a visit. The office will book it and tell the tenants.
- **Invoice.** Tap Invoices to make an invoice. You can add your own reference, a note, the products you bought and receipt photos. Tap "Tidy my wording" if you'd like your note written more clearly.

**Please remember:** for fire, gas or flooding, call 999 first.

If you have any trouble, reply to this email or call 0207 112 9163.

Thank you,
The Capital Rooms team

2. Sign in. Today's cleans are shown on the home screen.

**What you can do:**

- **Look up a house.** Tap a house to see its notes, its usual rhythm and past cleans.
- **Add notes for the house.** Note anything the next person should know, and tick notes off when they're done.
- **Invoice.** Tap Invoices to invoice your cleans for a period. Add your own reference, notes and any products you bought. Tap "Tidy my wording" if you'd like a note written more clearly.
