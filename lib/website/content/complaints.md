We are committed to providing a professional service to all our clients and customers. If things go wrong, we need you to tell us. That helps us improve our service and resolve issues as soon as possible.

If you have a complaint, please put it in writing (letter or email). We will then acknowledge it and respond within the timescales below. The whole process should take no longer than **8 weeks**.

We consider the needs of each person and, where appropriate, make reasonable adjustments for people who might be disadvantaged by their age, infirmity, disability, lack of knowledge, lack of linguistic or numeracy ability, economic circumstances, bereavement, or because English is not their first language.

## Stage 1: Your complaint

Put your complaint in writing, by letter or email, addressed to:

**Harry, Director**
Capital Rooms, Hoxton Mix, 66 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NA
Email: management@capitalrooms.co.uk

Include as much detail as you can: the issues you want considered, relevant dates, the names of any staff you dealt with, and any supporting evidence you can enclose or attach.

## Stage 2: Our acknowledgement

We will acknowledge your complaint and start our in-house complaints process.

**Timescale:** within 3 working days of receiving your complaint.

## Stage 3: Our investigation

Harry (or a relevant member of staff, where that applies) will investigate and send a formal written response. It will address each of your points, propose resolutions where appropriate, and set out our final response. Because of the size of the organisation, we are unable to carry out more than one formal investigation.

**Timescale:** we aim to respond within 15 working days of receiving your complaint.

## What you can expect from our reply

Our final reply sets out, in plain language:

- **What happened**, with the dates of each step taken, so you can follow the history.
- **What we accept** we could have done better. We will say so where it is true.
- **What we will do next**, with a named person responsible and a timescale for each outstanding item.
- **Who to contact**, including a direct number, if you have questions about the letter.

If a repair is still outstanding, we keep you informed while we wait for a part or a contractor, and we will not treat a delivered part as a finished repair. Changing contractor does not change our responsibility to see the work through.

We also review how outstanding repairs are tracked, so that delays are escalated sooner and responsibility for follow-up is clear.

## Stage 4: The Property Ombudsman

If you are still dissatisfied after our final viewpoint letter, you can refer your complaint to The Property Ombudsman:

- Web: [www.tpos.co.uk](https://www.tpos.co.uk)
- Email: [complaints@tpos.co.uk](mailto:complaints@tpos.co.uk)

You can only refer a complaint once we have sent our final viewpoint letter, or 8 weeks have passed since you first complained. The Property Ombudsman no longer accepts complaints by post. Referrals must be made online or by email.

**Timescale:** refer your complaint within 12 months of the date of our final viewpoint letter. There is no charge for making a complaint.
